Alcohol impairment remains a massive, critical crisis on public roads, directly contributing to over 30% of all traffic deaths in the U.S. Eliminating it is a mission we proudly support alongside long-standing partners like Mothers Against Drunk Driving (MADD). But our latest peer-reviewed research highlights a second, equally sobering truth that we cannot ignore: traffic fatalities rarely result from a single factor, and improving road safety requires examining conditions beyond driver behavior alone.
